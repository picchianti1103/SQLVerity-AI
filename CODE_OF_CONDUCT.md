# Code of conduct

SQLVerity AI contributors and maintainers are expected to keep project spaces welcoming, professional,
and focused on constructive technical collaboration.

We do not tolerate harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate
disruption, or publication of another person's private information. Disagreement is welcome when it
addresses ideas and evidence rather than people.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, and other participation that violates these
expectations, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary. Report
conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through the hosting platform. Reports will
be handled as confidentially as practical, with attention to the safety of the reporter.

These expectations apply in repository discussions and in public spaces where someone is formally
representing SQLVerity AI.
